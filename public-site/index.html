How a switch bonus works

  1. Pick an offer you qualify for. Each one has rules about accounts you already hold and bonuses you have had before.
  2. Open the new account and ask it to run a Current Account Switch from your old one. Choose a switch date at least seven working days ahead.
  3. Meet the conditions. Usually that means moving some Direct Debits across and paying in a set amount, sometimes salary.
  4. Wait for the bonus. Banks typically pay within a few weeks of the conditions being met; each offer says exactly when.
