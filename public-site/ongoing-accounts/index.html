Accounts that keep paying

A switch bonus is a one-off. A few accounts keep rewarding you afterwards. Whether they are worth keeping depends on what they ask for and what they cost, so we set out both.

Salary matching

Krak (an app account from Kraken) pays a percentage of each salary payment, set by your average balance over the last 30 days, and a separate percentage as cashback on card spending. The salary has to arrive from an employer, at least £2,500 a month, and salary above £25,000 a month is not counted.

Yearly member payments

Nationwide Fairer Share about £100 a year, not guaranteed

A yearly payment to Nationwide members with a current account that was open on 31 March and used in January-March, plus £100+ in a Nationwide savings account (or on a mortgage) in March. Paid for three years running (2026: about 4.4 million members, from 10 June). Nationwide decides each year with its May results. Virgin Money customers missed the 2026 round. Taxable as savings interest.
