How we make money

Some of the links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and then open an account, or sign up to a cashback site, we may be paid a commission by the provider or by an affiliate network.

What that means for you

  • It does not cost you anything. The price, the bonus and the terms are the same whether or not you arrive through our link.
  • It does not decide what we show. Offers are listed by size of bonus, using the same data for every provider, whether or not we are paid by that provider. We do not remove an offer because we do not earn from it.
  • We mark paid links. Affiliate links carry an “Ad” or “Referral link” label near the button, are tagged as sponsored in the page code, and are covered by a note like the one at the foot of each offer page.

Where the money comes from

  • Affiliate networks and providers. We may promote providers through affiliate programmes, and are paid when you take up an offer through a tracked link.
  • Referral schemes. TopCashback, Quidco and Krak run refer-a-friend schemes. If you join using our link, we may receive a reward, and in some cases so may you.

Our independence

Commissions do not buy a place on this site, a better ranking or a different description. The tables and guides are generated from the same structured data for every offer, and we correct errors when we find them. We are not a regulated adviser; see about us.
