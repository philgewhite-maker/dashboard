Club Lloyds: £200 switch bonus

Lloyds Bank pays £200 in total to people who switch to the Club Lloyds and move 3+ Direct Debits across.

What you need to do to get the bonus

  1. Move 3+ Direct Debits onto the new account (a full switch carries them across).
    The offer’s own wording

    Switch in 3+ Direct Debits; the switch must be started by 9 Dec.

Not sure what a test means? See the money-in guide and the Direct Debit guide.

When you are paid

PaymentWhen
£200Paid within 14 working days of the conditions being met

Who cannot get it

  • Not open to anyone who has received a Lloyds, Halifax or Bank of Scotland (either party on a joint account) switch bonus since 1 January 2023.
  • You must switch from a different banking group: not from Halifax and Bank of Scotland.
  • The switch has to be started by 9 December 2026.

Opening the account

Open online, by phone or in branch.

Ask Lloyds Bank to start the Current Account Switch from the account you are leaving. It takes seven working days and closes the old account; see how the service works.

Extras

  • A yearly reward: 12 months Disney+ (with ads), six cinema tickets, Coffee Club & Gourmet Society, or a magazine subscription
  • 6.25% regular saver on up to £250/month for a year

The terms in full

Show the offer’s conditions as extracted

£200 for switching in an account from a different bank (not Halifax/Bank of Scotland) with 3+ Direct Debits; the switch must START by 9 Dec. No previous switch bonus from Lloyds, Halifax or Bank of Scotland since 1 Jan 2023.

This is a summary of the offer’s terms and may be out of date; the bank’s own terms apply. Last checked 22 October 2026.

One bonus per banking group. Lloyds Bank is treated as one banking group, so you generally cannot collect bonuses from more than one of its accounts at a time. Also in this group: Lloyds Premier.
