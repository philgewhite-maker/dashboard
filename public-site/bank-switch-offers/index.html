How to read the table

  • Bonus is everything the offer pays if you meet every condition, added up. Some are paid in instalments over several months.
  • What it asks for shows the main tests: a salary test needs pay from an employer, a pay-in test needs a set amount paid in, and the Direct Debit count is how many must move across. See the money-in guide and the Direct Debit guide.
  • Start by is the date the switch must be started by, where the offer has one.

Offers with no salary test

6 of the 10 offers do not ask for salary paid in by an employer, so they can suit people who are between jobs, self-employed or retired. They may still ask for a pay-in or Direct Debits.
