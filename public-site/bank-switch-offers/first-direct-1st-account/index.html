First Direct 1st Account: £210 switch bonus

First Direct pays £210 in total to people who switch to the First Direct 1st Account and move 2+ Direct Debits across and pay in £1,000+.

What you need to do to get the bonus

  1. Move 2+ Direct Debits onto the new account (a full switch carries them across).
    The offer’s own wording

    Switch in an account with 2+ Direct Debits or standing orders within 45 days.

  2. Pay in £1,000+ within 45 days. The money has to stay at least 24 hours.
    The offer’s own wording

    Pay in £1,000+ within 45 days (dribs and drabs fine); one guide says each deposit has to stay at least 24 hours.

  3. Make 5+ debit card payments within 45 days.
    The offer’s own wording

    5+ debit card payments within 45 days.

  4. Log in to the bank’s app within 45 days.
    The offer’s own wording

    Log in to digital banking.

Not sure what a test means? See the money-in guide and the Direct Debit guide.

Who cannot get it

  • Not open to people who held a First Direct current account .

Opening the account

Open via the app (online for a joint account).

Ask First Direct to start the Current Account Switch from the account you are leaving. It takes seven working days and closes the old account; see how the service works.

Extras

  • Top customer service (90% rate it great), fee-free overseas spending, usually a £250 0% overdraft
  • 7% fixed regular saver on up to £300/month for a year

The terms in full

Show the offer’s conditions as extracted

£210 for switching in an account with 2+ Direct Debits/standing orders, paying in £1,000+, 5+ debit card payments and logging in to digital banking, all within 45 days. Never had ANY First Direct product; not opened an HSBC current account since 1 Jan 2018.

This is a summary of the offer’s terms and may be out of date; the bank’s own terms apply. Last checked 22 October 2026.

One bonus per banking group. HSBC and First Direct are treated as one banking group, so you generally cannot collect bonuses from more than one of its accounts at a time. Also in this group: HSBC Premier and HSBC Bank Account.
