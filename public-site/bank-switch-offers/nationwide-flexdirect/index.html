Nationwide FlexDirect: £175 switch bonus

Nationwide Building Society pays £175 in total to people who switch to the Nationwide FlexDirect and move 2+ Direct Debits across and pay in £1,000+.

What you need to do to get the bonus

  1. Move 2+ Direct Debits onto the new account within 28 days (a full switch carries them across).
    The offer’s own wording

    Switch in an account with 2+ Direct Debits within 28 days of opening.

  2. Pay in £1,000+ within 31 days.
    The offer’s own wording

    Pay in £1,000+ within 31 days of opening. Nationwide does not say whether it can be split, come from your own accounts, or has to stay.

  3. Make 1+ debit card payment within 31 days.
    The offer’s own wording

    Make at least one debit card payment within 31 days of opening.

Not sure what a test means? See the money-in guide and the Direct Debit guide.

When you are paid

PaymentWhen
£175Paid within 10 days of the conditions being met

Who cannot get it

  • Not open to anyone who has received a Nationwide switch bonus since 1 January 2021.
  • You must switch from a different banking group: not from Virgin Money.
  • Start the switch within 28 days of opening the account.

Opening the account

Open an account, then switch in within 28 days.

Ask Nationwide Building Society to start the Current Account Switch from the account you are leaving. It takes seven working days and closes the old account; see how the service works.

Extras

  • 5% interest on balances up to £1,500 for a year (about £75 a year)
  • Up to £120 a year cashback (£5 a month for £300+ of Direct Debits, £5 a month for £500+ of debit card spending) (about £120 a year)
  • 6.5% regular saver on up to £200/month for a year (about £84 a year)

The terms in full

Show the offer’s conditions as extracted

£175 for switching in an account with 2+ Direct Debits within 28 days of opening, then paying in £1,000+ and making at least one debit card payment within 31 days of opening. No Nationwide switch bonus since 2021 (a sole-to-joint switch is an exception); not switching from a Virgin Money account.

This is a summary of the offer’s terms and may be out of date; the bank’s own terms apply. Last checked 22 October 2026.
