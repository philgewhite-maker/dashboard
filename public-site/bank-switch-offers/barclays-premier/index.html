Barclays Premier: £600 switch bonus

Barclays pays £600 in total to people who switch to the Barclays Premier and move 3+ Direct Debits across and have £5,000+ salary paid in.

What you need to do to get the bonus

  1. Move 3+ Direct Debits onto the new account (a full switch carries them across).
    The offer’s own wording

    Switch in 3+ Direct Debits.

  2. Have £5,000+ salary paid in by an employer (a transfer from your own accounts does not count) in a month within 30 days.
    The offer’s own wording

    £5,000+ salary within 30 days, by Bacs from an employer.

  3. Have £5,000+ salary paid in by an employer (a transfer from your own accounts does not count) every month for 6 months.
    The offer’s own wording

    Keep £5,000+ monthly salary arriving for five more months. Miss a month and you can still qualify in later months.

  4. Make 10+ debit card payments in each of 6 months.
    The offer’s own wording

    10+ debit card payments in each of the next five months.

Not sure what a test means? See the money-in guide and the Direct Debit guide.

Who cannot get it

  • Not open to people who held a Barclays current account on or before 1 Oct.
  • Not open to anyone who has received a Barclays switch bonus before.
  • Income test: £75,000+ a year, or large savings with Barclays.
  • Single (sole) accounts only; the offer is not available on joint accounts.

Opening the account

Open a sole account in Barclays' app.

Ask Barclays to start the Current Account Switch from the account you are leaving. It takes seven working days and closes the old account; see how the service works.

Extras

  • Apple TV subscription included free (Blue Rewards; normally £10/month) (about £120 a year)
  • Possible £500 0% overdraft; fee-free spending abroad

The terms in full

Show the offer’s conditions as extracted

£450 upfront for switching 3+ Direct Debits and paying in £5,000+ salary within 30 days; a further 5 x £30 over the next five months. Needs £75,000+ a year (or large savings with Barclays). No previous Barclays switch bonus; no open Barclays current account on or before 1 Oct.

This is a summary of the offer’s terms and may be out of date; the bank’s own terms apply. Last checked 22 October 2026.

One bonus per banking group. Barclays is treated as one banking group, so you generally cannot collect bonuses from more than one of its accounts at a time. Also in this group: Barclays Bank Account.
