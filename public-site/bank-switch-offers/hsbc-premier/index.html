HSBC Premier: £500 switch bonus

HSBC pays £500 in total to people who switch to the HSBC Premier and have salary paid in.

What you need to do to get the bonus

  1. Have your salary paid in by an employer (a transfer from your own accounts does not count) each month.
    The offer’s own wording

    Salary paid into the account each month (HSBC does not say how many months, so every payday in the 70 days to payment is taken as required — 3 at least; whether self-employed income counts is not stated).

Not sure what a test means? See the money-in guide and the Direct Debit guide.

When you are paid

PaymentWhen
£500Paid within 70 days of the conditions being met

Who cannot get it

  • Not open to people who held a HSBC or First Direct current account since 1 January 2025.
  • Income test: £100,000+ a year (before tax), or £100,000+ saved/invested (no switch needed).
  • Start the switch within 90 days of opening the account.

Opening the account

Open HSBC Premier and switch an account in within 90 days.

Ask HSBC to start the Current Account Switch from the account you are leaving. It takes seven working days and closes the old account; see how the service works.

Extras

  • Worldwide family travel insurance (to age 69) and digital GP access

The terms in full

Show the offer’s conditions as extracted

£500 for earning £100,000+ a year (salary paid into the account each month) and switching in within 90 days, OR having £100,000+ saved/invested with no switch needed. No HSBC/First Direct current account held since 1 Jan 2025.

This is a summary of the offer’s terms and may be out of date; the bank’s own terms apply. Last checked 22 October 2026.

One bonus per banking group. HSBC and First Direct are treated as one banking group, so you generally cannot collect bonuses from more than one of its accounts at a time. Also in this group: HSBC Bank Account and First Direct 1st Account.
