NatWest Premier Reward: £500 switch bonus

NatWest pays £500 in total to people who switch to the NatWest Premier Reward and have salary paid in and pay in £15,000+.

What you need to do to get the bonus

  1. Have your salary paid in by an employer (a transfer from your own accounts does not count) each month.
    The offer’s own wording

    Salary paid in each month on the £100,000-earner route (NatWest does not say how many months, so every payday in the 120 days to payment is taken as required).

  2. Pay in £15,000+ within 90 days. The money has to stay at least 24 hours.
    The offer’s own wording

    Pay in £15,000 within 90 days of the switch completing, in one or several deposits that each stay at least 24 hours.

  3. Log in to the bank’s app within 90 days.
    The offer’s own wording

    Log in to the NatWest mobile app.

Not sure what a test means? See the money-in guide and the Direct Debit guide.

Who cannot get it

  • Not open to people who held a NatWest current account on or after 2 Sep.
  • Not open to anyone who has received a NatWest, RBS or Ulster Bank switch bonus before.
  • Income test: £100,000+ a year (before tax), or £100,000+ saved/invested, or a £500,000+ NatWest mortgage.
  • You must switch from a different banking group: not from RBS and Ulster Bank.
  • Single (sole) accounts only; the offer is not available on joint accounts.

Opening the account

Open the account and complete a switch.

Ask NatWest to start the Current Account Switch from the account you are leaving. It takes seven working days and closes the old account; see how the service works.

Extras

  • £9 a month cashback (net £7 after the £2 fee) (about £84 a year)

The terms in full

Show the offer’s conditions as extracted

£500 for earning £100,000+ a year (or £100,000+ saved/invested, or a £500,000+ NatWest mortgage) and paying in £15,000 within 90 days of the switch completing. No previous NatWest/RBS/Ulster Bank switch bonus; no NatWest account on or after 2 Sep.

This is a summary of the offer’s terms and may be out of date; the bank’s own terms apply. Last checked 22 October 2026.

One bonus per banking group. NatWest and Ulster Bank are treated as one banking group, so you generally cannot collect bonuses from more than one of its accounts at a time. Also in this group: NatWest Reward.
