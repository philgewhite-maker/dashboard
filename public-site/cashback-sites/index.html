Cashback sites compared for bank accounts

On top of a bank’s own switch bonus, cashback sites often pay a smaller fee for sending you to open an account. The amounts differ by site and change often, so it pays to compare them for the account you are opening.

How to make sure it tracks

  1. Pick one site for each application and do not mix.
  2. Start from the cashback site, search for the bank, and click through to its page in the same browser window you will use to apply.
  3. Turn off ad blockers for the click and use no other discount codes or links on the way.
  4. Apply in one go, in the same session, and do not start the application elsewhere first.
  5. Keep a record: a screenshot of the click and of the confirmation email.
  6. Check back after a few days that the cashback shows as pending, and chase the site with your evidence if not.

Common questions

Can I use a cashback site and the bank’s own switch bonus together?

Usually yes: the cashback is paid by the site for sending you to the bank, and the switch bonus is paid by the bank. Read both sets of terms. Some banks exclude customers who arrive through certain channels.

Why has my cashback not tracked?

Most often because the click-through was not made in the same browser session before applying, an ad blocker interfered, or the application was started somewhere else first. Tracking can take some time to show, so keep a screenshot of the click and the application.

Can I claim through two sites?

Treat one application as one site. Most terms do not allow the same purchase to be claimed twice, and a double claim risks both.

When is the cashback paid?

Cashback sites normally show it as pending first and confirm it after the bank confirms the account. The wait is set by the site and the bank.
